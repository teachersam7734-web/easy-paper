## Banner

One restrained editorial triptych with subtle gutters: crimson translucent scan plates and a hovering sheet of scholarly paper at left, a blue-gray detailed moon and crossing star orbits in the center, a polished straight sword and several flowing copper arrow trajectories at right. Premium practical studio lighting, real paper and metal texture, deep ink background throughout, interesting volumes and photorealistic material work. Group the visual action in a central horizontal band with enough top and bottom slack to crop to 3:1; preserve darkest clean negative space near the center for the large title “Easy Paper”. Render that exact title in high-contrast ivory sharply typeset modern editorial serif, one line, no other text, no fake interface, no badges, no watermark. Wide landscape output.
