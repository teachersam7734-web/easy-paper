# Prompt Vault

About 103 ready-to-use prompts. Each one has **Use when**, **Time**, the prompt itself (fill the `[BRACKETS]`), **Why it works** (tied to the science), and **Variations**. They grew out of the prompts in the *ADHD Study System* PDF, then were expanded, made more precise, and checked against the research in `../references/science/`.

## Ready-made study routes

| Route | Time | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| **First contact** with a new paper | 45 min | 01 triage → 01 mission → Review Guide p.1 (mind map) → 02 sprint × 2 → 03 blind recall |
| **Present it** next week | 3 × 60 min | Deep Dive → 05 Socratic tutor on each Hunt Question → 03 Feynman teach-back → 07 mock Q&A |
| **Oral exam** critique | 2 × 45 min | Critique Notes → 09 Critique drill → 07 mock oral exam → 03 sparring (skeptic card) |
| **Cram** in 2 hours | 120 min | Follow the Review Guide's game plan; use 09 Review Guide prompts for the quiz |
| **Language harvest** | 15 min per paper | Vocab Treasure → 09 vocab drill → add 3 items to your own writing this week |

## Rules for using AI as a guide
1. Ask it to ask you first (retrieval beats re-reading).
2. Paste the paper's text, or the Easy Paper PDF content, so it works from the source rather than its memory.
3. Ask for page anchors, and check one or two of them.
4. Keep each session time-boxed, and end on something you produced, not on "Do you understand?".
5. Follow your university's AI policy when the work is assessed.
