# Academic science: verified reference list

## How these were verified

- Every entry with a DOI was checked against Crossref through a reference-checker tool (DOI resolves, title and journal match). Items the checker flagged are noted.
- Several entries were also surfaced through the Consensus paper index, which supplied abstracts and effect sizes quoted in the science files.
- Crossref returned author lists only partly in the checks, so a few entries use "et al." where the full list was not confirmed. APA 7 lists up to 20 authors: complete these before formal publication.
- Books have no DOI unless noted. Books are cited from standard bibliographic knowledge and were not machine-checked.
- Nothing here is invented. If an item could not be confirmed it sits in the "Not verified" section at the bottom and is not used as evidence in the science files.
