# Verified references (academic: education and interventions)

Verification method: DOIs were matched against Crossref via a reference-checker tool in this session. Items marked "(DOI not retrieved)" were found through a literature search tool (Consensus) but not matched to a DOI; check before formal citation. ISBNs for books come from publisher or retailer listings seen this session, except where marked "from memory".

Year notes: some journals have an online-first year that differs from the print year; the print year is used where I was confident, with the DOI as the anchor.
