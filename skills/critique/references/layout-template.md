# Critique Notes layout template (3 to 5 pages)

| # | Block(s) | Content | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | cover (`meta.cover: "compact"`) | kicker "Critique Notes · <genre>"; chips: genre, "3 strengths · 4 weaknesses", "90-sec script" | top quarter of p.1 |
| 2 | `verdict` | the one-breath verdict + five ratings | 1 box |
| 3 | `preview` | 4 to 5 questions | 1 box |
| 4 | `nuggets` (title "What the paper says, in 4 lines") | 4 nuggets, neutral | 1 box |
| 5 | `section` "The paper in brief" | `p` lead with the yardstick sentence ("It's a [genre], so judge it as [job]."); 3 to 4 `chunk`s (summary beats) with one small visual each (`pipeline`, `table`, `stats`) | about 1 page |
| 6 | `section` "Strengths" | 3 to 4 `critique_card` kind strength, n = 1.. | about 0.7 page |
| 7 | `section` "Weaknesses and limitations" | 3 to 5 `critique_card` kind weakness with severity, ranked; optional one `callout` tone `warn` "Don't say this" for a tempting but unfair point that failed the fairness filter | about 1.2 pages |
| 8 | `section` "Say it in 90 seconds" | `script` (seconds: 90) + `callout` tone `key` with the memory hook | about 0.4 page |
| 9 | `qa` "If the examiner asks..." | 5 to 6 | about 0.5 page |
| 10 | `quiz` | 8 to 10 | about 0.5 page |
| 11 | answer key (auto) | | |

Rules:
- Cards are the content; no extra prose paragraphs in sections 6 and 7.
- Strength titles and weakness titles are short and memorable, and parallel within each section.
- Keep the authors' order in section 5; rank by severity in section 7.
- A `columns` block (2 columns) may hold short strength cards side by side if space allows.
