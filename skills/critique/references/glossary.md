# Critique craft glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Yardstick | The standard a paper is judged by, set by its genre and its own stated aims |
| Authors' contract | Purpose, scope and promises; the paper is judged against them first |
| Exhibit / anchor | The page, figure, number or quote that proves a point |
| Documented absence | Something verified to be missing after searching the paper for it and two synonyms |
| Fairness filter | Five checks before keeping a weakness: really absent? excluded on purpose? required by the genre? would readers miss it? about this paper in this field? |
| FAE | Feature, Anchor, Effect: the anatomy of a strength |
| CECA | Claim, Evidence, Consequence, Alternative: the anatomy of a weakness |
| Internal-consistency critique | Showing that the paper's own logic needs what is missing (the strongest kind) |
| Wish-list critique | "I wish they had covered X" (the weakest kind; usually cut) |
| Severity | major (affects the paper's main claim), moderate (affects a part or its usefulness), minor (presentation, conventions) |
| Remedy | A small, realistic fix: a table, a paragraph, a second example, a disclosure |
| Verdict | The balance of merits and limits, plus how to use the paper |
| Say-it line | The spoken version of a point, at most 25 words |
| Signpost | Spoken structure words: "two strengths", "the biggest problem", "finally" |
