# Agiel references

Agiel uses original biconcave red-cell geometry, real perspective projection, depth ordering, and directional surface shading. The pointer nudges camera parallax. Selecting an evidence topic moves the scanner beam, and the reader sees that topic's real description. The crimson cells provide an editorial metaphor. Their number and appearance carry no medical or quantitative meaning.

- Cursor trails and soft fluid motion reference [Pavel Dobryakov’s WebGL Fluid Simulation demo](https://paveldogreat.github.io/WebGL-Fluid-Simulation/) and its [MIT repository](https://github.com/PavelDoGreat/WebGL-Fluid-Simulation).
- [Liquid Ether](https://www.reactbits.dev/backgrounds/liquid-ether) and the [React Bits repository](https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits) provided interaction references for restrained idle movement and pointer response.
- [Red Blood Cells](https://github.com/zehraworks/red-blood-cells) provided a reference for 3D specimen staging and mouse interaction.
- The local HTML God motion and single-file references guided reduced-motion defaults, bounded drawing work and offline packaging. The reference sites supply visual and interaction ideas. The delivered scanner is original Canvas code.

Rajdhani Bold, Orbitron and Inter come from the workspace fonts. Their OFL notices accompany the embedded files.
