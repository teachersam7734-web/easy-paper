# Confessor

The lunar observatory pairs a dark celestial opening with a pale study surface. Selecting a study lens changes the illustrated Moon's lighting and orbit, reveals the destination and item count, and opens the corresponding paper section. Moving the pointer shifts the viewpoint and nearby stars. The lighting presets illustrate the study lenses. The procedural stars and orbits do not represent an observed sky.

- [Oh My Luna](https://ohmyluna.com/en/) and [Nightscape](https://weeblabs.github.io/Nightscape/) supplied immersive lunar composition references. [Nightscape’s MIT repository](https://github.com/WeebLabs/Nightscape) documents its separate treatment of the lunar map, sky and atmosphere.
- [Codrops’ immersive lunar storytelling guide](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/04/20/interactive-storytelling-for-the-web-building-immersive-stories-with-timelines-3d-and-layered-scenes/) informed the study lens, which changes illumination while keeping the same observable object.
- The bundled lunar albedo image comes from the [NASA Scientific Visualization Studio CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/), using LRO camera data. Credit goes to NASA’s Scientific Visualization Studio. [NASA image and media guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)
- HTML God’s local motion, 3D and single-file guides informed interaction and packaging. The final spherical projection, lighting, parallax and control logic use original Canvas code.

EB Garamond, its italic, Inter and IBM Plex Mono are from the workspace fonts. Their OFL notices accompany the embedded files.
