# Review Guide layout template (exactly 4 sheets)

`meta.cover: "compact"` puts a slim header at the top of page 1 (it shares sheet 1's space). Each `sheet` is 270 mm tall; the renderer reports overflow. Budgets are approximate heights.

## Sheet 1. "The big picture" (sub: "Read this first · 10 min")
| Block | Budget |
|---|---|
| `preview` (4 items) | 28 mm |
| `mindmap` (height_mm about 115 to 125) | 125 mm |
| `callout` tone `key`, title "If you remember one thing" | 18 mm |
| `nuggets` (3 to 5) | 35 mm |
(The compact cover takes about 38 mm.)

## Sheet 2. "Must-knows (1/2)" (sub: "The 3-star items · 35 min")
3 to 4 `chunk`s with id "★★★ 1" and so on. Each has question, answer, a 40 to 110 word body and one compact visual (`definition`, small `table`, `pipeline`, `compare`). Use `columns` (2) for short chunks.

## Sheet 3. "Must-knows (2/2)" (sub: "2-star items, numbers, hooks · 30 min")
- 2 to 4 `chunk`s or `cards` for the 2-star items
- `stats` or `table` "Numbers worth memorising"
- `cards` (cols 3, tone gold) "Memory hooks"
- `compare` or `table` "Don't mix these up"

## Sheet 4. "Exam ready" (sub: "Test yourself · 45 min")
- `qa` "Likely exam questions" (4 to 5)
- `studyplan` "Your 2-hour game plan" (120 min)
- `quiz` (8 items; the hint says the key is below)
- `answer_key` (place it explicitly at the end of sheet 4 so it stays on the page)

Default 2-hour plan:
| min | task |
|---|---|
| 10 | Page 1: answer the preview questions with a guess; read the mind map aloud |
| 35 | Page 2: the 3-star items; cover each short answer and say it back |
| 25 | Page 3: 2-star items, numbers, hooks |
| 15 | Blind recall: redraw the mind map from memory, then check it |
| 20 | Page 4: answer the likely exam questions aloud, then take the quiz |
| 15 | Fix: reread only what you missed; retake the missed quiz items |
