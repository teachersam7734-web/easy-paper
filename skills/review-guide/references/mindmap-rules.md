# Mind-map rules (page 1)

The user's favourite feature of the earlier guides. Keep it, and make it readable cold.

- **Centre:** the paper's core idea in at most 4 words (for example "Mixed Methods = 3rd Paradigm"), plus a 6 to 10 word subline (the message, compressed).
- **Branches:** 5 to 7, following the paper's logic rather than its headings. Typical order: Problem or Why → What it is / Definition → Method or Evidence → Core content (1 to 3 branches) → So what → Limits.
- **Labels:** 1 to 3 words plus a matching icon (search, book-open, flask-conical, layers, lightbulb, triangle-alert, users, clock, git-compare, scale, target).
- **Leaves:** 2 to 4 per branch, each at most 8 words, as noun phrases or tiny claims. Use **bold** for the key term in a leaf. Add page anchors only on 3-star leaves.
- **Numbering:** branches are numbered 1 to n in reading order (the renderer does it). Left column reads top to bottom, then the right column.
- **Colours:** the renderer's palette is fine. Override a branch colour only to tie it to a must-know card.
- **Spec:** `{"type":"mindmap","center":"...","sub":"...","height_mm":120,"branches":[{"label":"...","icon":"...","leaves":["...","..."]}]}`. The map should take about 45 to 55% of page 1.
- **Test:** cover everything except the map. Can a classmate explain the paper in 60 seconds from the map alone? If not, fix the branches, not the leaves.
