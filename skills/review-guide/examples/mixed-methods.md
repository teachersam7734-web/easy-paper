            HUNT QUESTION ★★★ 1 p. 123 9 min
            How do Johnson and colleagues define mixed methods research?

         SHORT ANSWER A researcher or team combines qualitative and quantitative elements (viewpoints, data
         collection, analysis, inference) for breadth and depth of understanding and corroboration.

            VERBATIM DEFINITION
            Mixed methods research (general definition)

             “Mixed methods research is the type of research in which a researcher or team of researchers combines
             elements of qualitative and quantitative research approaches (e.g., use of qualitative and quantitative
             viewpoints, data collection, analysis, inference techniques) for the broad purposes of breadth and depth of
             understanding and corroboration.”
             Johnson, Onwuegbuzie & Turner, 2007, p. 123

            IN PLAIN WORDS You mix qual and quan pieces on purpose, to see more and to check what you find.

        Three parts. Who: a researcher or a team. What: elements of both approaches, not only data. Why: breadth and
        depth of understanding, plus corroboration. A study mixes within one study; a program mixes across related studies
