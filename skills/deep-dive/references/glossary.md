# Deep Dive craft glossary

| Term | Meaning in this mode |
|---|---|
| Message | What the authors are trying to say, in 1, 3 or 10 sentences |
| Centre of gravity | The part of the paper that carries most of its weight; it gets the most space |
| Roadmap | The 4 to 6 step diagram of the paper's logic or procedure, built to be put on a slide |
| Hunt Question (HQ) | The question a chunk answers; written the way a curious classmate would ask it |
| Short answer | The answer to the HQ in at most 45 words, shown before the explanation |
| Presenter's arc | Why this paper, what it asks, how, the core content, what it means, where it stops |
| Canonical definition | A definition whose exact wording matters; quoted verbatim with a printed page |
| Gap types | Time, evidence, population or context, method, concept, practice (scout-protocol.md) |
| Relation tags | extends, challenges, replicates, updates, fills-gap: how a newer work relates to the paper |
| Talk track | The 60-second spoken outline of the paper |
| Outside the paper | Anything from the field references or the Scout; always labelled |
| Page budget | 5 to 10 pages and at most half the original's page count |
