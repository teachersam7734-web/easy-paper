# Presenter kit (the Coach, Deep Dive)

The student will stand in front of a professor and peers. The kit gives them a spine to talk from and answers to the questions they will get.

## 60-second talk track (`script`, 6 to 9 lines)
1. Hook: the problem in one sentence ("Everyone says 'mixed methods', but nobody agreed what it meant.").
2. The paper and its move ("Johnson and colleagues asked 21 leaders and analysed 19 definitions.").
3. to 5. The 2 or 3 core ideas, one line each, using the paper's labels.
6. The payoff ("Their composite definition calls mixed methods the third paradigm.").
7. One honest limit ("It's a consensus of a network, not a census of the field.").
8. The bridge to discussion ("So the question for us is...").
Rules: spoken register; at most 20 words per line; signpost words (first, the key idea, so); no citations inside the lines except names.

## Likely questions (`qa`, 5 to 6)
Cover: (1) a definition check, (2) a "why did they..." method question, (3) a "so what for teachers or researchers" question, (4) a weakness (from dossier §12 or scout gaps), (5) a "has anyone tested this since?" question (from scout.md), and optionally (6) a connection to an SLA theory.
Model answers: answer first, then one reason, then one example or page. 2 to 3 sentences.

## If you blank
One recovery line the presenter can always fall back on: "The core idea is X; the evidence is Y; the open question is Z."
