# Scout protocol: supplements, newer work and gaps

## 1. Find the gaps first (before searching)
Look for these gap types in the main paper (dossier sections 7, 9, 12, 13 and the conclusion):
| Gap type | Question |
|---|---|
| **Time gap** | Has the field moved since publication (new tools, new policies, newer models, a decade of studies)? |
| **Evidence gap** | Are the claims supported by perception data only, a small or local sample, or a narrative review without a search method? |
| **Population or context gap** | Which learners, languages, proficiency levels or countries are missing? |
| **Method gap** | Which design would test the claim properly (control group, longitudinal, mixed methods, replication)? |
| **Concept gap** | Is a key construct undefined, contested, or defined differently elsewhere? |
| **Practice gap** | Recommendations without procedures: who does what, and how? |

Pick the 3 to 5 most important. Each must be anchored to the paper (a page, or a documented absence after searching paper.txt).

## 2. Search
- Tools, in order of preference: scholarly connectors if present (Consensus, Elicit, Semantic Scholar, Wiley, alphaXiv for arXiv), then web search with terms from the gap plus "systematic review", "meta-analysis", "2023", "2024", "2025".
- Prefer: peer-reviewed journal articles from the last 5 years; systematic reviews and meta-analyses; well-cited replications; policy documents (COPE, publisher policies) when the paper is about policy.
- 4 to 8 works in total. Relevance beats fame.

## 3. Verify (non-negotiable)
For every work: find the DOI or the publisher page, and confirm authors, year, title and venue. If a reference checker tool is present (for example a Crossref-based checker), run it. A work you cannot verify is dropped, not flagged.

## 4. Write scout.md
```markdown
## Gaps
| # | Gap (type) | Where in the paper | Why it matters for the presenter |
## Newer work
| # | APA 7 reference | DOI/URL | Relation (extends/challenges/replicates/updates/fills-gap) | So-what in 1-2 sentences | Gap # |
## Beyond-the-paper cards (3-5)
- Title: ... | Text (<=50 words) | Tone: green (supports) / pink (challenges) / violet (updates)
## Verification log
| Work | How verified |
```
The Designer turns this into the researcher section. Everything in it is outside the paper and is labelled so.
