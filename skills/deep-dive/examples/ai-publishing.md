            HUNT QUESTION HQ1 PDF pp. 1-4 5 min
            Why does this paper exist, and what kind of paper is it?

                  Generative AI now writes at an average human level, and misconduct reports are rising.
         SHORT ANSWER
         So the authors review recent literature on AI ethics in academia, with practical advice on oversight. It
         is a narrative review with no research questions.
        Tools like ChatGPT now write "at a level that either matches or surpasses the quality of an average human
        writer" PDF p. 2 . That matters in academia, where writing is how faculty are judged, and misconduct
        reports "have surged in recent years" PDF p. 2 . The gap is no clear guidance for authors, reviewers and

        editors using generative AI.
        The abstract promises "a review of recent interdisciplinary literature" with two foci: scholarly misconduct
        and necessary oversight for "writing, editing, and reviewing" PDF p. 1 . There are no research questions, so
        this is not an empirical study. It mixes summary with how-to advice, a sample policy and demonstrations. It
        calls itself a "chapter" PDF p. 2 and was posted on arXiv in June 2024. No search method is reported, so do

        not call it a systematic review.

            VERBATIM DEFINITION
            Generative AI
             “a type of artificial intelligence that generates new data/content rather than only classifying or predicting

             outcomes based on input data”
             Lund et al., 2024, PDF p. 2
            IN PLAIN WORDS AI that makes something new, not only AI that sorts or predicts. A spam filter classifies
            emails, so it is not generative; ChatGPT writing a paragraph is.
